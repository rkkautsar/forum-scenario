The chat has 180 members now. Questions disappear beneath photos and side conversations, and the same useful answers get typed every week.

Priya has been trying a tiny forum with a handful of regulars. Posts and comments work. It is pleasantly boring, which is exactly what she wanted. She asks you to help maintain it before inviting the rest of the group on Friday.

> “People need to find yesterday's answers, save a thread for later, and fix their own typos. Votes would help the useful posts stand out. We don't need to become Reddit by Friday.”

A few volunteers have sent contributions. Two people independently tackled voting, and two tried search. Other contributions are things their authors personally wanted. You have a working application to explore, not just isolated functions. Run it, change demo users, and trace a request from the browser to SQLite.

Your job is to decide what belongs in this release. A contribution can be well intentioned and still need changes. A small optional improvement can be worth accepting. You may also decline something useful when it does not fit the release.
